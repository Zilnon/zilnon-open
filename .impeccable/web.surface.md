# Public research reading surface

Mode: Read.

Reader task: move from the latest publication to a company or research category, understand what a report says and what it cannot establish, and inspect its original sources.

First viewport: quiet publication masthead, explicit synthetic notice when applicable, a large two-line editorial title, compact edition time, watchlist links, and the start of the latest brief. No promotional CTA, invented evidence, or market-dashboard conventions.

Signature interaction: report citations navigate to identifiable source entries; archive filters compose search, category, and symbol and expose a usable empty state. Filter URLs can be shared.

States: published complete and unreviewed; synthetic visibly labeled; no reports; no company reports; no filter matches. Invalid publications fail the build instead of silently substituting demonstration content.

Acceptance: static routes link together; public JSON is validated before rendering; all source text is escaped; evidence, generation, and publication clocks are separate with SGT display and UTC datetime attributes; desktop/mobile are readable and keyboard usable.

Build path: code-led, following the user's approved clean and clear editorial direction. No imagery is required for a text-first research publication.
