# Zilnon Open visual system

The public research journal uses a warm paper surface (#faf9f5), dark ink (#232a27), restrained forest links (#244d42), and quiet rules (#d8dcd4). Reading is the primary task. The approved direction is clean, professional, and minimal, implemented directly in code.

Georgia heads the local serif stack for article titles and editorial section headings; the system sans stack supports navigation, metadata, labels, and controls. No external fonts, remote imagery, ornamental charts, or imported product CSS. The mark is an original line-drawn Z.

The broad editorial grid has a dated rail, a readable title and summary column, and a single clear report link. Fine horizontal rules separate reports. Company names and symbols are navigational text, not dashboard tiles. A soft, full-width notice identifies synthetic content. Status is ordinary readable metadata.

A maximum 1160px shell gives room for report indexes. Long-form reports use a narrow contents rail and a 730px reading column; at mobile widths these become one column. Body text remains 16–17px and headings wrap naturally. Navigation is exposed at every size rather than hidden behind a menu.

Links carry visible focus rings; forms use explicit labels and native controls. Archive filters update a polite live count and preserve the filter in the URL. Citation anchors highlight the cited source. That focused highlight is the only authored motion and respects reduced-motion preferences.

The same rules apply to latest brief, watchlist, company records, reports, archive, and methodology. Content truth takes priority: source attribution, unreviewed status, and three distinct timestamps remain visible. Never use mock prices, returns, or chart-like decoration to imply analysis.
